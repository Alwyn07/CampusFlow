## 🎯 What is CampusFlow?

**CampusFlow** is a modern campus management platform designed to bring
academic, examination, faculty, and student activities into one unified
digital environment.

Instead of managing different campus operations separately, CampusFlow
provides a centralized platform for:

**Students · Faculty · Examinations · Marks · Results · Attendance ·
Hall Tickets · Notifications · OCR Automation · AI**
